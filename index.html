About

I’ve sat in your customer’s chair

Most recently, as a senior litigation paralegal, I co-designed and support my firm’s enterprise legal-AI pilot — serving on the AI committee, facilitating sessions and office hours, integrating tools like Microsoft Copilot and Filevine’s LOIS into daily work, and earning trust from staff to partners. I know what it takes to move a skeptical firm from access to a daily habit.

Before that, for more than a decade I ran a boutique firm end to end — client relationships, billing and receivables, and the renew-or-replace decisions across our software stack. So a book of business, health metrics, and a revenue conversation aren’t abstractions to me. I’m moving into customer success deliberately, because helping people get lasting value from legal AI is the work I want to do.

How I’d run a mid-market book

Consultative where it counts — productized so it scales

A mid-market book of 40+ firms can’t be high-touch everywhere, so I do both: act as the prescriptive advisor inside each account, and productize the routine into one-to-many playbooks, cadences, and self-serve enablement that lift the whole book at once. One simple loop, run everywhere.

01

Drive adoption

Prove it’s safe, then get every account to a first win fast — and on to a daily habit, with guided onboarding, office hours, and use-case templates that scale across the book.

02

Evidence the value

Turn usage into documented ROI — time saved, workflows touched — and bring it to a crisp business review the sponsor can defend internally.

03

Watch health, act early

Monitor adoption and sentiment across the book; catch churn signals weeks early and intervene with a targeted play, not a renewal-day scramble.

04

Expand on proof

When value is real, grow it — more seats, a second practice group, a new workflow — so net revenue retention climbs past 100%.
