About

I’m moving into customer success deliberately, because helping people get lasting value from legal AI is the work I want to do.

How I’d run a scaled book

Value, on repeat — reserved high-touch for the moments that move the number

Scaled CS can’t be high-touch everywhere, so I productize the routine and spend judgment where it counts: a simple loop run across the whole book.

01

Drive adoption

Get every account to a first win fast, then to a daily habit — with self-serve onboarding, office hours, and use-case templates that scale.

02

Evidence the value

Turn usage into documented ROI — time saved, workflows touched — and bring it to a crisp value review the sponsor can defend.

03

Watch health, act early

Monitor adoption and sentiment across the book; catch churn signals weeks early and intervene with a targeted play, not a renewal-day scramble.

04

Expand on proof

When value is real, grow it — more seats, a second practice group, a new workflow — so net revenue retention climbs past 100%.
